Wei Wang received the B.S. degree in Mechatronics Engineering from Chang'an University and the M.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU), where he is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Mechanical Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Zhiqiang Cai. From September 2025 to August 2026, he is a government-sponsored joint-training Ph.D. student at Politecnico di Milano, conducting collaborative research under the supervision of Prof. Enrico Zio. He has also worked at ZTE Corporation and iFLYTEK as a data analyst and an algorithm engineer, respectively.

His research interests include system reliability optimization and prognostics and health management. He received the 2024 National Scholarship for Doctoral Students and leads a project supported by the NPU Doctoral Dissertation Innovation Fund. In recent years, he has published more than ten papers in international journals, including *IEEE Transactions on Reliability* and *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*. He also serves as a reviewer for *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*, *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*, *Measurement*, and *Neurocomputing*. His research has received the Best Student Paper Award at the Asia-Pacific International Symposium on Advanced Reliability and Maintenance Modeling (APARM) and the Excellent Paper Award at the IEEE Global Reliability and Prognostics and Health Management Conference.
